Ka Jingpule Man Ka Sngi
✝

31 August 2026

" Bad U la kren ha ki, bun kiei kiei ruh ha ki pharshi, U da ong: “Ha khmih, u nongbet u la leit noh ban bet "

Matthew 13:3

Ia ka jingmut (concept) jong ‘u nongbet’, ‘u symbai’ bad ‘ka lyngkha’ la ju pyndonkam ha kiba bun ki rukom ha kane ka ‘secular world’. Bad la pyndonkam ruh ha ka ‘spiritual world’ kum ha kane ka pharshi u nongbet.

1 ‘U nongbet’ u thew nyngkong eh ia U Trai Jisu, u thew ruh ia ki synran jong U kiba bteng ia ka kam jong U, bad u thew ruh ia ka Balang kaba iai ialap bad trei Mission lyngba kine ki spah snem kiba la leit noh bad kaban dang iai trei tad haduh ka jingwan arsien jong U Trai Jisu.

2 ‘U symbai’ u thew ia ka Ktien U Blei kaba U Trai Jisu hi U la kren, U la ialap bad U la hikai. Dei ia kane ka Ktien ba kiba ngeit ha U Trai Jisu ki dei ban ialap bad ban hikai.

3 ‘Ka lyngkha’ ka thew nyngkong eh ia ki Jiw kiba im ha ki sngi jong U Trai Jisu. Ka thew ruh ia ki Jiw bad ia ki Jentil kiba im hadien ki sngi jong U, bad ka thew ruh ia kane ka pyrthei, kaba long ka lyngkha trei tad haduh ka jingwan arsien jong U Trai Jisu.

KA JINGPYRKHAT ✦

Kum kiba ngeit, ngi don ka jingkitkhlieh ban iathuh ia kane ka Ktien sha kiba bun ki bym pat iohsngew shaphang jong ka. Ka ‘cyber space’ lane ka Internet ka dei ka lyngkha kaba heh bad kaba iar kaba ngi dang ioh ban pynlong ia ka kum ka lyngkha trei mission. To ngin trei hok ha ka katba ka lad ka dang plie ia ngi.
