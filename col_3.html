Ka Jingpule Man Ka Sngi
✝

02 September 2026

"Kiwei pat de ki la hap halor ki jaka maw ha ka bym don bun khyndew; kumne kumne ruh ki la mih, naba kam long jylliew ka khyndew: Haba ka la ieng ka sngi te, ki la shit tyrkhong eh; bad naba kim don thied, ki la iap ryngkhiang.""

Matthew 5:5

Shi paralok ki ia nia, uwei u ong, ‘Me te kum u nar mo? uba shu dait thah beit’. Uta uwei pat u la ong, ‘Me pat kum u aluminium, katba dang don ding, me khluid palat, tang lip ka ding me la kloi ban dait thah, nga te kum u nar, suki ban khluid tangba slem ban dait thah’.

Na baroh ki synran, u Petros u dei uba shongshit tam bad uba don beit ha khmat ha khmat. Ngi lah ban ong ba u dei uba long ‘emotional’ bha. Haba u iohi ia U Trai Jisu ba U iaid halor ka duriaw, u la shongshit ban iaid lem bad U hynrei tang khyndiat por u la jan ngam noh. Kumjuh ruh ha shuwa ba U Trai Jisu Un shah, u la ong ba lada ka dei ia u ban iap lem bad U, un ym len noh ia U, hynrei u la len noh ia U. U Petros u la long teng kum kata ka khyndew kaba don halor ki jaka maw, kaba jyndong eh ba u la pdiang da ka jingshongshit ia ki jinghikai jong U Trai Jisu hynrei kim pat shym la suh thied ha ka dohnud jong u.

KA JINGPYRKHAT ✦

Ka lah ban jia ia ngi ruh haba ki persecutions kin wan, ba kum u Petros, ngin len wat ia ka jingngeit Khristan hi. Kumno te ngin jubab ha kaba kum kata ka por, lada don ba kylli, ‘Are you a Christian?’
