September 5th, 2026

Ki nongrwai bad ki nongtem kiba pawnam ha ka pyrthei ki ju wan ban perform ha ki Concerts ha Shillong. Baroh baroh ki ia kwah ym tang ban iohi hynrei khamtam eh ban iohsngew ia ki. Hynrei ym baroh kiba ioh ia ka lad ban iohi bad iohsngew ia ki. Pynban don kiba shu wan tang ban bishar bad ban peit kai ia ki, ki riam kumno bad ki iaid kumno.

U Trai Jisu, ha ka dkhot kaba 17 U ong, Naba shisha Nga ong ha phi, ba bun ki nongiathuhlypa bad ki riewhok ki la kwah ban iohi ia kiei kiei ruh ba phi iohi, te kim shym la iohi; bad ban iohsngew ia kiei kiei ruh ba phi iohsngew, te kim shym la iohi bad la iohsngew.

SCRIPTURE

"Uba don ki shkor ban sngew, to un sngew."

Mathaios 13:9

Ki jinghikai jong U Trai Jisu ki long kiba ym pat don ha ka Testament kaba Rim, ki nonghikai kiba la hikai kum ma U. Kita kiba ha kaba mynbarim ki thrang eh ban iohi ia U Trai Jisu, Uba long U Messiah, ban iohsngew ia ki jinghikai jong U hynrei kim shym la iohi bad la iohsngew.

Ki jinghikai jong U Trai Jisu ki long kiba kordor haduh katta katta. Hynrei kiba na ki Pharisi kim shym la wan da ka jingthrang ban iohsngew ia ki jinghikai kiba kordor jong U, hynrei ki wan tang ban ioh ban kem daw bad ban wad dong pyrshah ia U.
