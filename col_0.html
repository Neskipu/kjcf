Ka Jingpule Man Ka Sngi
✝

30 August 2026

“ [3] Bad U la kren ha ki, bun kiei kiei ruh ha ki pharshi, U da ong: “Ha khmih, u nongbet u la leit noh ban bet. [4] Haba u dang bet te, khyndiat ki la hap harud lynti, bad ki sim ki la wan, bad ki la bam ïa ki. [5] Kiwei pat de ki la hap halor ki jaka maw ha ka bym don bun khyndew; kumne kumne ruh ki la mih, naba kam long jylliew ka khyndew: [6] haba ka la ïeng ka sngi te, ki la shit tyrkhong eh; bad naba kim don thied, ki la ïap ryngkhiang. [7] Kiwei pat te ki la hap hapdeng ki dieng shiah; te kita ki dieng shiah ki la mih, bad ki la ïakjap ïa ki. [8] Kiwei pat te ki la hap ha ka khyndew ka babha, bad ki la sei soh, kiba shispah shah, kiba hynriewphew shah, kiba laiphew shah ruh. [9] Uba don ki shkor ban sngew, to un sngew.”

Matthew 13:3-9

Phi kwah ne em ba phin long kiba seisoh ha ka jingim jong phi kum ki khun Khristan? Phi kwah ne em ba ha kano kano kaba phi leh, la ha ka jingpule jong phi, la ha ka jingtrei jingktah jong phi, lane la ha kano kano kaba phi leh, phin long kiba jop? Ngi lah ban shem ia ka jubab ha kane ka pharshi u nongbet.

Ka daw balei ba U Trai Jisu U kren ia kane ka pharshi u nongbet ka long namar ba U shem ba ka Ktien kaba U kren, kam ioh ban suhthied ha ka jingim jong kiba bun kiba iohsngew ia ka bad kumta kim long kiba seisoh ha ka jingim. Ha kane ka pharshi, U Trai Jisu U jied saw jait ki khyndew ban pynsyriem ia ki saw jait ki jinglong ka dohnud:

1 U Trai Jisu U pynsyriem ia kita kiba eh dohnud kum ki Pharisi kum kata ka khyndew harud lynti ha kaba u symbai um ioh wat tang ban ngam shapoh khyndew. Ki Pharisi ki dei kita kiba bud ia U Trai Jisu kylleng ki jaka bad ki dei kita kiba kwah ban iohsngew aiu U Trai Jisu U kren. Hynrei ki dei kita kiba shu wad tang ban ioh wohdong wohnia ia U, ban kem daw pyrshah ia U bad ban kyntait ia ki jinghikai jong U.

2 U Trai Jisu U pynsyriem ia kita kiba lehse ngi lah ban ong kiba shu shongshit tang shipor ha ka jingsumar ia ka Ktien jong U, kum kata ka khyndew kaba jyngdong kaba don halor u mawsiang ba u jingthung na u symbai um ioh ban suhthied. U Petros u dei uta uba shongshit tam na ki synran jong U Trai Jisu bad uba ha khmat ha khmat. U la lah ban iaid halor um shipor hynrei u la jan ngam, u la bud ia U Trai Jisu kumba u la kular skhem hynrei u la len noh ia U.

3 U Trai Jisu U pynsyriem ia kita kiba lehse ngi lah ban ong kiba don hapdeng jong ki jingpynshoi bad ki jingthala jong kane ka pyrthei, ba ki long kum kata ka khyndew kaba don ki diengshiah kiba kjap noh ia u jingthung uba mih na u symbai. U Judas u dei uba ngop ha ka jingkwah spah kat haduh ban die ia U Trai Jisu, bad lehse kata ka long ka jingthmu jong u haba u la wan ban bud ia U Trai Jisu. Kata ka jingkwah spah ka kjap noh ia ka jinglong synran jong U.

4 Bad U Trai Jisu U pynsyriem ia kita kiba ka dohnud jong ki ka long kaba sngur bad kaba bha kum kata ka khyndew kaba jylliew bad kaba sboh ha kaba u jingthung u ioh ban mih na u symbai bad ha kaba une u jingthung u sei ia ki soh kiba laiphew, kiba hynriewphew bad kiba shispah shah. Don ki khynnah kiba rit kiba ju don ryngkat bad la ki kmie ki kpa kiba bud ia U Trai Jisu. Kine ki khynnah ki iohsngew bad ki shahshkor bha ia ki jinghikai jong U Trai Jisu. Kine ki long kum kane ka khyndew kaba bha, naba ki long kiba kloi ban pdiang da ka jingsngur ka dohnud ia ka Ktien jong U. Bad namarkata U Trai Jisu U ong ba lymda ngi long kum ki khynnah kiba rit, ngin ym ioh rung sha ka hima ki bneng.

Ngin sa ia puson kham bniah ia kawei pa kawei na kita ki saw tylli ki khyndew lyngba jong ka taiew ka ban wan, ryngkat bad ka jingpuson lyngkot shaphang uta u nongbet, uta u symbai bad kata ka lyngkha. Ka KJCF ka la pynsuk haduh katta katta da kaba pynpoi ia kine ki Jingpuson ba phi lah ban pule man la ka sngi ia ki lyngba ki smartphones ki jong phi, kata ha kano kano ka por kaba phi lait, la ka long ha ka por kaba phi ioh ‘off’, lunch time, haba phi shong kali ne hangno hangno haba phi don. Lada ngi lah ban ai por 10-15 minit ba ngi bam ia ka jingbam ban tei ia ka met ka phad, kan ym dei ban long kaba shitom ban ai por 1-2 minit ba ngin bam ia ka jingbam ban tei ia ka jinglong jingim kaba kynja mynsiem, naba kine ki jingpuson ha ki sngi pohia kim dei satia kiba jrong, naba ki long tang hapoh 300 kyntien. Hooid, ha ka jinglong briew wat ban peit ia ki messages ruh ngi la ngiah lypa namar la bun than, bad kata ka lah ban jia ba phi lah ban shu iaid lait noh ia kine ki jingpuson khlem da pule ia ki. Kum ka KJCF ngim kwah ba phin leh kumta ia kine ki jingpuson kiba la pynkhreh kyrpang na ka bynta jong phi. Ka KJCF ka angnud bad ka thmu ban iai pynmih ia kine ki Jingpuson kiba man la ka sngi. To phi ruh kum ki nongpule, to iai pule tista bad ngam kham jylliew sha ki jingshisha na ka Ktien U Blei, kum kata ka khyndew kaba jylliew bad kaba bha, bad to phin duwai ruh ba U Blei Un iarap ia phi ban im katba kum ka Ktien jong U, khnang ba ka jingim jong phi kan long kaba seisoh.

KA JINGPYRKHAT ✦

Ka KJCF ka long kum une u nongbet, haba ka bet ia uta u symbai, uba long ka Ktien U Blei, kata lyngba kine ki jingpuson kiba man la ka sngi. Ka jingkylli ia ngi ki nongpule, khamtam ia phi ki khun samla, ka long phi kut jingmut ne em ban pule tista bad pynleit jingmut bha ia kine ki jingpuson na ka Ktien U Blei kiba man la ka sngi?
