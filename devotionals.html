Ki Devotionals kiba shim na ka Ktien U Blei

Ka KJCF Bangalore ka pynmih ia kine ki Jingpuson na ka Ktien U Blei kiba man la ka sngi.

Ka jingthmu jong kine ki Jingpuson ka long ban iarap ia ngi ban pyrkhat bad ban pyniasoh ia ka Ktien U Blei bad ka jingim jong ngi man la ka sngi.

Ka KJCF Bangalore ka kyrpad ia kiba pule ba lada ki don kano kano ka jingbymshai, kano kano ka jingkynthoh kaba ki kwah, lane kino kino ki jingkylli halor jong kine ki Jingpuson, kin sngewbha ban pyntip sha ngi.

Ki jingkylli ne jingkynthoh lah ban phah sha: kjcfbangalore@gmail.com

Sngewbha Kynmaw!

Wat la katta ruh ka KJCF Bangalore ka buh ia ka 'Disclaimer' ba kine ki jingthoh baroh kin long tang na ka bynta ka 'Private circulation' hapoh ka jylli treikam jong ka bad kam shim jingkitkhlieh shabar na ka jylli treikam jong ka.
